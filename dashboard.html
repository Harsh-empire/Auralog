About AuraLog

AuraLog blends registration, project tracking, and mentor collaboration into one nebula-inspired workspace.

Inside Mission Control you can:

  • Publish projects with AI-backed summaries and snippet highlights.
  • Log daily progress, blockers, and errors so mentors spot patterns fast.
  • Surface doubts and review threaded responses from peers or AuraLog AI.
  • Curate a lightweight profile that travels with your device token.

Every panel is tuned for low-friction updates so your team stays future-ready.

Need Help?

Quick tips for staying in sync and unlocking the full dashboard.

  1. Use the Projects tab to share builds; attach at least one snippet for richer AI context.
  2. Drop progress updates daily — blockers flagged here fuel mentor outreach.
  3. Hold on to the device token shown in Profile; it authenticates future edits.
  4. Switch themes under Settings to match your workspace lighting and mood.
  5. If something misbehaves, ping support@auralog.com with steps to reproduce.

Still unsure? Toggle back to the About tab or explore the README for deployment and API docs.
